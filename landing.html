02

Resistance emerges across multiple molecular targets.

Review the biological mechanisms represented in the workspace.

Reverse transcriptase

NRTI and NNRTI mutations alter nucleotide incorporation or inhibitor binding, often creating recognizable pathways such as M184V, K65R, TAMs and NNRTI pocket substitutions.

Protease

Major and accessory substitutions reshape the protease active site and compensate for fitness costs, making mutation patterns more informative than isolated substitutions.

Integrase and capsid

INSTI resistance often follows Q148, N155, Y143 or related pathways. Capsid inhibitor resistance adds a newer target represented in the workspace.
